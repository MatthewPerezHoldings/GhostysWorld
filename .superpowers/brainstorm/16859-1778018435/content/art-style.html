What art style for Ghosty's World?

This is the biggest remaining decision — it sets the vibe and how much asset work each level needs.

🌳   🐕‍🦺
🦴  🐩
📬
16-bit pixel sprites

A. Cute pixel art (16-bit)

Vibe: Stardew Valley, classic Zelda. Crisp pixel sprites, bright colors, charming.

Pros: nostalgic, fast to produce per level, scales well on phone & desktop, easy to make Ghost & Poppy recognizable.

Cons: needs a little pixel-art sprite work for each character (I can use a free asset pack as the base).

🎨
Hand-drawn cartoon
thick outlines, flat colors,
wobbly animations

B. Cartoon (vector/flat)

Vibe: Adventure Time, Untitled Goose Game adjacent. Bold outlines, flat colors, exaggerated proportions.

Pros: very expressive — Ghost's giant goofy ears, Poppy's wagging tail, the toot animation will land. Scales infinitely (SVG).

Cons: more design work per character; needs clean vector art (I can prompt Midjourney/DALL-E for sprite sheets, or use simple geometric shapes I draw in code).

🚪
🐕‍🦺    🐩
📬
all emoji, all the time

C. Emoji / symbolic

Vibe: charmingly minimalist. Emoji + simple geometric shapes for the world. Like a digital board game.

Pros: fastest possible to ship — I can build the entire game with zero custom art. Looks consistent on every device.

Cons: less "wow" factor; emoji rendering varies between OSes; harder to nail Ghost-the-character vs generic 🐕‍🦺.

📸
Real photos of Ghost & Poppy
cut out + animated as
cardboard-puppet sprites

D. Real photos of the dogs (cut-out style)

Vibe: South Park / Don't Hug Me I'm Scared. Real photos of Ghost & Poppy cut out, slid around as flat puppets on a stylized yard background.

Pros: maximally personal — it's literally THE Ghost. Unique. Funny. Great for sharing on social.

Cons: needs you to send me ~5–10 photos of each dog at different angles. More fiddly to animate.

My pick: D — real photos of Ghost & Poppy as cut-out puppets. The whole point of this game is "MY dogs." Showing real Ghost beats any sprite, and the cardboard-puppet look is funny on purpose. Backup pick: A — pixel art, if you'd rather not deal with collecting photos. Pick whichever sounds more fun to you, not whichever is "easier" — I'll handle the work.
